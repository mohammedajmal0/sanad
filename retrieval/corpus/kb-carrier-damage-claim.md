# Filing a carrier damage claim

## Deadline

File the claim with the carrier within 7 days of delivery. Claims filed later are rejected by all four carriers.

## What you need

The RMA number, the customer's photos of the damage and the packaging, the signed delivery note, and the order's weight.

## Steps

Complete claim form CL-2 in the logistics portal and attach the evidence. The logistics coordinator submits it to the carrier.

## Liability

Carrier liability is capped at SAR 50 per kilogram unless the shipment was declared with a higher value. Najm still credits the customer in full under the Returns and Damaged Goods Policy; any difference is a cost to Najm.

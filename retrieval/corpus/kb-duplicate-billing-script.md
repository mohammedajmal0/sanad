# Call script: customer billed twice

## Opening

"Thank you for letting us know, and I'm sorry for the confusion. Could you read me both invoice numbers so I can check them now?"

## Checking

Confirm that both invoices bill the same order number. If they do, the second invoice is a duplicate. Check whether either has been paid.

## Promise

"I can confirm the second invoice is a duplicate. We have stopped all reminders for it. If you have not paid it, please ignore it; we will cancel it. If you have paid it, we will issue a credit note within 5 business days and refund you or offset it against your next invoice, whichever you prefer."

## After the call

Log the case in the CRM with the category "billing" and send finance both invoice numbers the same day.

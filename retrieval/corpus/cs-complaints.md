# Complaint Handling and Escalation Policy

## Response times

Every customer complaint must be acknowledged within 4 business hours and resolved within 3 business days. Complaints from key accounts are acknowledged within 2 business hours.

## Escalation

If a complaint is not resolved within 2 business days, the account manager escalates it to the regional sales manager. If it is still open after 5 business days, the regional sales manager escalates it to the head of customer service, who calls the customer personally.

## Recording

Every complaint is logged in the CRM as a case with a category (billing, delivery, product quality, service) and the customer's own words. A case is closed only after the customer confirms the fix.

## Billing complaints

For billing complaints, customer service involves finance on the same day. Duplicate billing follows the Duplicate Invoice Handling Procedure.

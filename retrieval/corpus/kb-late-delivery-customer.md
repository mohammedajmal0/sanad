# Late delivery: what to tell the customer

## Before you call

Check the promised date and the current status in the ERP, and the carrier's latest estimate. Check whether a regional advisory, such as a port congestion advisory, applies.

## What to say

Apologise, give the new estimated delivery date, and explain the cause in one sentence without blaming the carrier by name. Tell the customer they can request a late-delivery credit under the Delivery Service Level Policy: 2% of the order value per business day late, up to 10%.

## Urgent orders

If the customer cannot wait, logistics can re-ship urgent items with another carrier. The account manager requests this from the logistics planner; Najm pays the extra freight.

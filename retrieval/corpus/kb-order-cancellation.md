# Cancelling an order

## Before shipping

An order can be cancelled free of charge until it ships. The account manager cancels it in the ERP. If the order was already invoiced, the invoice is cancelled at the same time.

## After shipping

A shipped order cannot be cancelled. The customer can refuse delivery or return the goods under the Returns and Damaged Goods Policy, and a restocking fee may apply.

## Custom orders

Custom orders cannot be cancelled once production has started. The account manager must tell the customer this before the order is confirmed.

# Delivery Service Level Policy

## Promised delivery dates

Every order gets a promised delivery date when it ships. The promise depends on the destination region:

- Riyadh: 2 business days after shipping.
- Jeddah, Dammam and Medina: 4 business days after shipping.
- Abha: 5 business days after shipping.

Business days are Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays.

## What counts as late

A shipment is late when it is delivered after its promised date. A shipment that has not been delivered yet is not counted as late or on time, because its outcome is unknown. The late rate is therefore the number of late deliveries divided by the number of delivered shipments, never by all shipments.

## Customer credit for late deliveries

When a delivery is late, the customer may request a credit of 2% of the order value for each business day late, capped at 10% of the order value. The credit is issued as a credit note with reason code LATE. Customers must request it within 30 days of delivery.

## Targets

The company target is a late rate below 3% in every region, measured monthly.

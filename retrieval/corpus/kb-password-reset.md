# Password reset and account lockout

## Lockout rule

An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlocks by itself after 15 minutes.

## Resetting your own password

Go to the self-service reset portal at reset.najm.local, confirm your identity with your registered authenticator app, and choose a new password. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. Passwords expire every 180 days.

## When self-service does not work

Call the service desk. The agent verifies your identity with your employee number and a call-back to your registered mobile number before resetting the password. Service desk agents never ask for your current password.

# Returns and Damaged Goods Policy

## Damaged goods

Customers must report damaged goods within 14 days of delivery, with photos of the damage and of the packaging. Customer service opens a return authorisation (RMA number) and the logistics team files a carrier damage claim. Damaged goods are replaced or credited in full, with no restocking fee.

## Returns of unwanted goods

Unopened goods in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 10% applies. Opened goods, custom orders and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## Process

1. The customer contacts their account manager or customer service.
2. Customer service issues an RMA number. Returns without an RMA number are refused at the warehouse.
3. The customer ships the goods back, or Najm collects them for orders above SAR 10,000.
4. The warehouse inspects the goods within 3 business days of receipt.
5. Finance issues a credit note with reason code RET.

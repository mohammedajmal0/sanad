# Looking up a customer account and its contacts

## Finding the account

Search the CRM by account name or account ID. Each account belongs to one region (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Medina or Abha) and has an owning account manager.

## What you will see

The customer 360 view shows the account's segment, number of orders, total order value, last order date, number of contacts and number of open invoice issues. Account managers see only the accounts in their own region. Ops managers see every region.

## Contact details

Contact email addresses and phone numbers are personal data. Account managers see them in full. Other roles see them masked as three asterisks. If you need a contact's details and you are not the account manager, ask the account manager.

# Customer Personal Data Policy (PDPL)

## Scope

This policy applies the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) to the personal data of customer contacts: names, job titles, email addresses and phone numbers.

## Principles

- Collect only the contact data needed to serve the account.
- Show personal data only to employees who need it for their role. Account managers see their customers' contact details; other roles see them masked.
- Keep customer contact data for 5 years after the account closes, then delete it.

## Requests from individuals

A contact may ask to see, correct or delete their personal data. Customer service must answer within 30 days. Requests are logged in the CRM.

## Breaches

A suspected personal data breach must be reported to the IT security team immediately. The data protection officer decides within 72 hours whether the regulator must be notified.

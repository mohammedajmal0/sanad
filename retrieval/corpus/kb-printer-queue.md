# Print jobs stuck in the queue

## Symptoms

Documents are sent to a floor printer but never print, and the queue shows "Error - printing" or "Spooling".

## Fix

1. Cancel all jobs in the queue.
2. Open Services, find "Print Spooler", and restart it.
3. Remove and re-add the printer from the IT portal printer list. Floor printers are named by building and floor, for example RUH-HQ-3F.
4. Print a test page.

If several users on the same floor are affected, the printer itself is the problem: report it to the service desk with the printer name and do not restart your laptop.

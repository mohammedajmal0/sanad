# Customer disputes an invoice amount

## First checks

Compare the invoice with the customer's purchase order and the signed delivery note. Most disputes come from a price change the customer did not accept, a partial delivery invoiced in full, or a duplicate invoice.

## Stopping reminders

Set the dispute flag on the invoice in the ERP. A dispute flag stops payment reminders for 30 days while the dispute is investigated.

## Resolution

If the customer is right, issue a credit note with the matching reason code. If the invoice is correct, send the customer the purchase order and delivery note as evidence and remove the dispute flag. Every dispute must be resolved within 30 days.

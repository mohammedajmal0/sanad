# Jeddah port congestion advisory (Q3 2026)

## What is happening

From July 2026, congestion at Jeddah Islamic Port is delaying inbound containers. Red Sea Cargo routes most Jeddah deliveries through the port, so its Jeddah deliveries are arriving several days after the promised date. The other carriers use road freight from Riyadh and Dammam and are not affected.

## Impact

Jeddah's late-delivery rate rose sharply in Q3 2026, while other regions stayed below 3%. Most of the late Jeddah deliveries are Red Sea Cargo shipments.

## What to do

- Book urgent Jeddah orders with Najd Freight instead of Red Sea Cargo until further notice.
- Tell Jeddah customers about possible delays when they order.
- Late-delivery credits still apply to customers. The congestion is a declared event, so Red Sea Cargo is not penalised for these delays under the Carrier Performance and Penalty Policy.

## Expected end

The port authority expects congestion to ease by the end of October 2026. Logistics will update this advisory when the late rate returns to normal.

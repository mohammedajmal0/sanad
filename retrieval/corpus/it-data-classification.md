# Information Classification and Handling Policy

## Classes

All company information belongs to one of four classes:

- Public: approved for anyone, such as the product catalogue.
- Internal: for employees only, such as this policy.
- Confidential: for named teams only, such as invoices, prices and margins.
- Restricted: the most sensitive, such as customer contact details, national ID numbers and bank details.

## Handling rules

Confidential and Restricted information may only be stored in approved company systems. Restricted information must never be pasted into external AI tools, personal email or messaging apps. Approved internal AI assistants that run inside the company's data platform may use it under the same access rules as the user.

## Labelling in the data platform

Tables and columns holding Restricted personal data carry the tag "pii". Tables with financial details carry the tag "confidential". Access to tagged data is reviewed every quarter.

# How to issue a credit note in the ERP

## Who can do it

AR supervisors draft credit notes. Approval follows the limits in the Credit Note and Refund Policy, and the ERP routes the draft to the right approver by amount.

## Steps

1. In the ERP, open Accounts Receivable, then Credit Notes, then New.
2. Enter the original invoice number. The customer and the invoice lines fill in automatically.
3. Choose the reason code: DUP for a duplicate invoice, LATE for a late-delivery credit, RET for a return, PRC for a pricing error.
4. Enter the amount to credit and attach the evidence, for example both invoice numbers for a duplicate.
5. Submit for approval. Drafts above SAR 5,000 go to the finance manager automatically.

## After approval

The ERP posts the credit note and emails the customer a PDF copy. If the customer had already paid, finance arranges the refund or the offset.

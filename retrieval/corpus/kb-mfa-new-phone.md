# Moving MFA to a new phone

## Before you change phones

If you still have the old phone, open the IT portal, choose "Security info", and add the authenticator app on the new phone before removing the old one.

## If the old phone is lost

Call the service desk. After verifying your identity, the agent issues a temporary access pass. It is valid for 8 hours and lets you sign in once to register the authenticator app on the new phone.

## Lost or stolen company phone

Report it to the service desk immediately so the device can be wiped remotely.

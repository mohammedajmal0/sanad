# Mailbox full and email archiving

## Limits

Each mailbox holds 50 GB. At 49 GB you receive a warning, and at 50 GB you can no longer send email.

## Automatic archiving

Email older than 2 years moves to your online archive automatically every weekend. The archive has no size limit and is searchable from Outlook.

## Freeing space now

Empty the Deleted Items folder, save large attachments to your OneDrive and delete them from the email, and move old project folders to the online archive by hand.

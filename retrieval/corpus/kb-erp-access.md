# Requesting access to the ERP

## How to request

Submit the "ERP access request" form in the IT portal. Choose the role you need, not the access of a colleague. Your line manager approves the request, then the ERP owner for that module approves it.

## Roles

- AR clerk: create and view invoices; cannot issue credit notes.
- AR supervisor: everything an AR clerk can do, plus cancel invoices and draft credit notes.
- Logistics planner: view orders and shipments, update delivery dates.
- Read-only analyst: view all modules, change nothing.

## Timing

Approved requests are completed within 2 business days. Access for contractors expires automatically after 90 days unless it is renewed.

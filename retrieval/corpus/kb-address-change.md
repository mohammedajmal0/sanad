# Changing a delivery address

## Before dispatch

The account manager can change the delivery address in the ERP at no cost until the order's status is Booked.

## After dispatch

Once the carrier has collected the order, the address can only be changed by a carrier redirect. A redirect costs SAR 75 per shipment, charged to the customer, and adds 1 business day to the promised date.

## Security check

Address changes requested by email must be confirmed by a phone call to a contact already registered on the account. This protects against delivery fraud.

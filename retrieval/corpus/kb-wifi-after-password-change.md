# Laptop cannot join office Wi-Fi after a password change

## Symptoms

After changing your password, your laptop no longer connects to the office network NAJM-CORP, although your phone does.

## Cause

The laptop still holds your old password for the Wi-Fi network, and the network rejects it.

## Fix

1. Connect to the guest network NAJM-GUEST or use a network cable.
2. Lock and unlock the laptop with the new password so it updates its stored credentials.
3. Open Wi-Fi settings, choose "Forget" on NAJM-CORP, and connect again with your new password.

If the laptop shows a certificate warning, do not accept it. Report it to the service desk instead.

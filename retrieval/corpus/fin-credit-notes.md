# Credit Note and Refund Policy

## Purpose

A credit note reduces what a customer owes Najm Supply Co. It corrects a billing error, compensates a late delivery under the Delivery Service Level Policy, or refunds returned goods. This policy sets who may approve a credit note and how fast it must be issued.

## Approval limits

Every credit note needs a second person to approve it before it is posted. The approver depends on the amount, including VAT:

- Up to SAR 1,000: the account manager's team lead.
- Above SAR 1,000 and up to SAR 5,000: the regional sales manager.
- Above SAR 5,000: the finance manager. Credit notes above SAR 5,000 always need finance manager approval, even when the cause is a clear billing error.
- Above SAR 50,000: the finance manager and the chief financial officer.

An AI assistant may draft a credit note, but it may never approve or post one.

## Reason codes

Each credit note carries one reason code: DUP for a duplicate invoice, LATE for a late-delivery credit, RET for returned goods, PRC for a pricing error, and OTH for anything else, which needs a written explanation.

## Timing

Credit notes are issued within 5 business days of the approval. If the customer has already paid the incorrect amount, finance refunds by bank transfer within 10 business days, or offsets the amount against the next invoice if the customer agrees in writing.

## Records

The credit note must reference the original invoice number. Finance keeps credit notes and their approvals for 10 years.

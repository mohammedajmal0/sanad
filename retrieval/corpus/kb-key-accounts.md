# Handling key accounts

## What a key account is

A key account is a customer in the enterprise segment with more than SAR 2 million in orders over the last 12 months. Key accounts are listed in the CRM with the segment "enterprise" and a key account flag.

## Service level

Key accounts have a named account manager and a backup. Their complaints are acknowledged within 2 business hours, and their credit notes are prioritised by finance.

## Terms

Key accounts may have Net 45 payment terms when the finance manager approves them in the contract.

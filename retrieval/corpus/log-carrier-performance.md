# Carrier Performance and Penalty Policy

## Carriers

Najm ships through four contracted carriers: Gulf Line, Najd Freight, Sahara Express and Red Sea Cargo. Logistics reviews each carrier's late rate per region every month.

## Thresholds

- A late rate above 5% in any region for one month triggers a written performance review with the carrier within 10 business days.
- A late rate above 10% in the same region for two consecutive months allows Najm to move that region's volume to another carrier without notice.
- The carrier pays Najm a penalty equal to the customer credits Najm issued for its late deliveries, up to 15% of that month's freight invoice.

## Exceptions

Delays caused by declared port congestion, customs inspections or weather alerts from the National Center of Meteorology are excluded from penalties for the period of the declared event, but they still count in the late rate that managers see.

## Escalation

The logistics manager owns carrier relationships. Account managers must not negotiate with carriers directly.

# Remote Access and VPN Policy

## Who may connect

Employees and approved contractors may access internal systems from outside the office only through the company VPN client, GlobalConnect, on a company-managed laptop. Personal devices may not connect to the VPN.

## Security requirements

- Multi-factor authentication is required for every VPN login.
- Idle VPN sessions disconnect after 30 minutes without activity.
- Split tunnelling is disabled: all traffic goes through the VPN while it is connected.
- The GlobalConnect client updates itself automatically. Users must not block or roll back updates without IT approval.

## Support

VPN problems are reported to the IT service desk with the category "VPN". During a known incident, IT publishes a knowledge base article with the workaround and links all related tickets to it.

# Payment Terms and Credit Hold Policy

## Standard terms

Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date (Net 30). Key accounts may have Net 45 terms when the finance manager approves them in the customer's contract. Payment is by bank transfer to the account printed on the invoice. Najm never changes its bank details by email.

## Reminders

The ERP sends payment reminders at 7, 21 and 45 days past the due date. A disputed invoice or one under a dunning hold receives no reminders until the hold is lifted.

## Credit hold

An account with any invoice more than 60 days overdue goes on credit hold. New orders for that account are accepted but not shipped until the overdue amount is paid or the regional sales manager approves a release. Each release is valid for one order only.

## Credit limits

Each account has a credit limit set by finance at onboarding and reviewed every year. Orders that would take the open balance above the limit need the finance manager's approval before they ship.

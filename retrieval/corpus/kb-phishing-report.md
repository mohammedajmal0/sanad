# Reporting a suspicious or phishing email

## Warning signs

Urgent requests for payment, a sender address that does not match the company name, requests to change bank details, and links to sign-in pages are common signs of phishing. Najm never changes its bank details by email.

## What to do

Do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook. It sends the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox. The security team responds within 1 hour during business hours.

## If you already clicked

Report it to the service desk immediately and change your password from a different device. Clicking is not a disciplinary matter; reporting late is the bigger risk.
